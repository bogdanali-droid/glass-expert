## SEGMENT 1: COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE (CRE)
**Primary Contact:** Architects, General Contractors  
**Average Deal Size:** €100k-500k  
**Sales Cycle:** 6-9 months  

### ELEVATOR PITCH (30 seconds)
```
"Glas Expert is Europe's leading architectural glass supplier, 
now serving USA projects. We specialize in custom solutions — 
curved glass, ceramic-printed facades, and ultra-fast lead times 
(4-6 weeks vs. typical 8-12 weeks). Perfect for projects where 
design differentiation matters and schedule is tight."
```

### THE PROBLEM WE SOLVE
✓ **Long lead times frustrate architects** — Typical USA suppliers: 8-12 weeks  
✓ **Limited customization** — Most supply commodity glass only  
✓ **Quality concerns** — Inconsistent manufacturing standards  
✓ **Project delays** — Compressed timelines from owner/developer pressure  

### GLAS EXPERT ADVANTAGES (3-5 min presentation)
| Feature | Benefit | Why It Matters |
|---------|---------|----------------|
| **Curved glass capability** | Custom shapes for signature facades | Architectural differentiation = competitive edge for design firms |
| **European manufacturing** | Premium quality + consistency | Reduces rework costs + schedule risk |
| **4-6 week lead time** | Faster than standard suppliers | Compressed schedules = on-time delivery |
| **ASTM/EN certified** | Pre-qualified for USA projects | Architect confidence + faster approvals |
| **Ceramic-printed glass** | Custom branding possibilities | Commercial appeal for mixed-use projects |

### SAMPLE PROJECT CONVERSATION
```
ARCHITECT: "We need custom curved glass for a 12-story office 
facade. Current quote is 12 weeks, timeline is compressed."

YOU: "Perfect opportunity for Glas Expert. Our Timişoara factory 
specializes in complex curved work — we can deliver in 6 weeks. 
What's the radius and m² volume? Let me run preliminary specs."

[Gather: radius, glass type, coating requirements, quantity]

YOU: "Here's what makes us different: [Glas Expert European story]. 
We've done similar projects for [case study example]. Let me send 
technical specs and pricing — should be 15-20% faster AND better 
price due to manufacturing efficiency."
```

### PRICING POSITIONING
- **Standard market:** €80-130 per m² for curved glass
- **Glas Expert position:** €95-120 per m² (competitive, plus speed advantage)
- **Margin:** 40-50% target

**Objection: "Your price is higher than [competitor]"**
```
YOU: "I hear you. Let's look at total cost of ownership. If 
their lead time causes a 3-week schedule slip, that's €50k-100k+ 
in contractor demobilization costs. Our 6-week delivery saves you 
that risk. Quality: European manufacturing = 99%+ first-pass, 
reduced rework. What's worth more to you: €5k in glass savings or 
schedule certainty?"
```

### COMPETITIVE POSITIONING
- vs. Guardian, PPG (USA suppliers): "We're faster + more custom"
- vs. Chinese suppliers: "Quality + reliability + compliance"
- vs. European suppliers (existing): "We're now in USA = shorter logistics"

### CONVERSATION STARTERS
- "What's your typical curved glass lead time challenge?"
- "How critical is schedule certainty for your current pipeline?"
- "Have you had rework issues with glass suppliers before?"
- "What glass types do your buildings typically require?"

### NEXT STEPS (Closing the conversation)
```
IF INTERESTED: "Let me send you specs + a case study. Can we 
schedule 20 minutes next week to discuss your project details?"

IF UNCERTAIN: "No pressure. Here's my contact info. When you're 
ready to spec glass, I'd love to be in the conversation."

IF OBJECTING: [Handle objection] "Does that address your concern? 
Would a sample or reference call help?"
```

## 📞 UNIVERSAL OBJECTION HANDLING

### OBJECTION 1: "Your price is too high"
```
YOU: "Fair question. Let me explain our positioning:

[Pick relevant]:
- CRE: Fast delivery saves schedule costs + quality = rework reduction
- Hospitality: Design differentiation = higher project value
- Healthcare: Compliance certainty = reduces approval risk/timeline
- Education: Durability = lifecycle cost advantage

What's the most important cost factor for your project: upfront 
price, schedule certainty, or lifetime value?"

[Match answer to value prop, offer discount only if answer 
justifies it (usually volume or framework deal)]
```

### OBJECTION 2: "We already have a glass supplier"
```
YOU: "Completely understand. We're not trying to replace them — we're 
offering an alternative for specialty work:
- Curved glass (if you don't have this capability)
- Custom finishes (ceramic-printed, etc.)
- Fast lead times (for compression scenarios)
- European quality (for premium projects)

Could we be a good fit for your specialty/custom work?"

[Listen for specific pain points with current supplier]
```

### OBJECTION 3: "I've never heard of Glas Expert"
```
YOU: "That's totally fair — we're newer to the USA market 
(launched March 2026). But we've been making architectural glass 
in Europe for 20+ years. 

[Pick relevant]:
- CRE: We've done facades for [major European buildings]
- Hospitality: Luxury hotels across Europe use our glass
- Healthcare: Hospital systems across Europe specify us
- Education: University campuses use our glass

We're bringing that European quality/experience to USA projects. 
Reference calls? We can arrange calls with [European project 
leaders in your segment]."
```

### OBJECTION 4: "Lead time is too long for my project"
```
YOU: "How compressed is your timeline? [Listen]

Our strength: We do 4-6 weeks. If that doesn't work, let me 
understand the constraint:
- Is it a hard construction deadline? (We can sometimes expedite)
- Is glass late in your critical path? (We can prioritize)
- Do you need samples first? (We can send samples in 1 week)

Let me see if we can make the timeline work. Worst case, we reference 
a faster supplier. But I think we can help."
```

### OBJECTION 5: "We need [very specific certification]"
```
YOU: "No problem — what's the spec? [Listen carefully]

[One of three responses]:
1. We have it: "We're certified to that standard. Let me send 
   documentation."
2. We can get it: "We're not currently, but we can. Timeline is 
   [X weeks]. Does that fit your project?"
3. We can reference: "We don't do that specific cert, but here's 
   who we'd recommend. [Reference]"

[Don't fake credentials — credibility is everything]
```
