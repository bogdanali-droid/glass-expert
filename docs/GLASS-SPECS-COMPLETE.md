## 🔧 GLASS TYPES & SPECIFICATIONS

### 1. TEMPERED GLASS (Safety Glass)
**What:** Heat-treated glass that breaks into small, safe fragments  
**Properties:**
- 4-5x stronger than annealed glass
- Safe breakage pattern
- Cannot be cut after tempering
- Thermal shock resistant (up to 200°C differential)

**Applications:**
- Building doors + windows (safety requirement)
- Automotive
- Shower enclosures
- Balustrades
- Any load-bearing application

**Standards:**
- ASTM C1048 (USA)
- EN 12150 (Europe)
- Tempered glass must break into pieces <50mm

**Cost Factor:** +40-60% vs. annealed glass  
**Lead Time:** 2-4 weeks (depends on size/shape)

**Glas Expert Capability:** ✅ Full tempering facility + proprietary process

---

### 2. LAMINATED GLASS (Safety + Strength)
**What:** Multiple glass layers bonded with plastic interlayer (PVB)  
**Properties:**
- Holds together when broken (security + safety)
- Sound dampening (-30dB typical)
- UV filtering (depending on interlayer)
- Energy efficient (thermal layer options)
- Can be curved after lamination

**Applications:**
- Architectural facades
- Security applications
- Sound-controlled areas
- Hurricane-resistant windows
- Skylights + overhead glazing

**Standards:**
- ASTM C1172-24 (Laminated architectural flat glass)
- EN 11096 (Europe)
- Must withstand 1kg impact test

**Interlayer Options:**
- **PVB:** Standard, good sound dampening
- **SGP:** Higher strength (structural)
- **Acoustic:** Enhanced sound insulation

**Cost Factor:** +60-150% vs. annealed (depends on interlayer)  
**Lead Time:** 3-6 weeks

**Glas Expert Capability:** ✅ Advanced laminating with proprietary interlayers

---

### 3. CERAMIC-PRINTED GLASS (Design + Durability)
**What:** Screen-printed ceramic enamel fused to glass at high temperature  
**Properties:**
- Permanent design (won't fade)
- Scratch-resistant
- Can print complex patterns + colors
- Food-safe (suitable for kitchens)
- Artistic applications

**Applications:**
- Interior design + branding
- Kitchen backsplashes
- Office partitions (opaque privacy)
- Artistic installations
- Promotional applications

**Printing Process:**
1. Design creation
2. Screen preparation
3. Printing on glass surface
4. Kiln firing (700-800°C) to fuse enamel
5. Quality inspection

**Cost Factor:** +100-300% (depends on design complexity)  
**Lead Time:** 4-8 weeks (includes design approval + kiln time)

**Glas Expert Advantage:** Romania's first digital ceramic printer (installed 2015) + proprietary design capabilities

**Design Specifications:**
- Minimum line width: 1.5mm
- Maximum resolution: 600 DPI
- Color matching: Pantone system
- Coverage: Full glass or partial (gradient options)

---

### 4. CURVED GLASS (Architectural Feature)
**What:** Glass bent to custom shapes during heat treatment  
**Properties:**
- Structural strength maintained
- Any curve possible (geometric or artistic)
- Complex shapes for architectural features
- Aesthetic + functional

**Applications:**
- Cylindrical facades
- Curved stairs
- Dome skylights
- Artistic installations
- Automotive (special applications)

**Constraints:**
- Thickness limited (3-10mm typical)
- Minimum radius dependent on thickness
- Cannot be laminated after bending (usually)
- One-way strength (best when compression side is outer)

**Cost Factor:** +200-500% vs. flat glass  
**Lead Time:** 6-12 weeks (custom molds + heating equipment)

**Glas Expert Capability:** ✅ Proprietary curved glass process + mold library

## 📋 INTERNATIONAL STANDARDS & COMPLIANCE

### ASTM Standards (USA Market) ⭐ CRITICAL FOR USA SALES
| Standard | Description | Critical For |
|----------|-------------|--------------|
| **ASTM C1036-16** | Flat glass specifications | All architectural glass |
| **ASTM C1048** | Tempered glass properties | Safety applications |
| **ASTM C1172-24** | Laminated glass (architectural) | Laminated products |
| **ASTM E1300** | Load resistance of glass | Wind load calculations |
| **ASTM E1886** | Performance of building glazing | Hurricane/impact resistance |
| **ASTM E2112** | Insulating glass unit (IGU) | Energy efficiency |

**USA Compliance Required:** All products must meet ASTM for building code acceptance

---

| Standard | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **EN 12150** | Tempered soda lime silicate glass |
| **EN 11096** | Laminated glass |
