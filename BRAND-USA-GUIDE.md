## 2. BRAND VOICE — TONE OF VOICE

### Voice Pillars

**1. Technical-Confident**  
We know glass at a molecular level. We speak as experts, not salespeople. We use correct technical terminology without dumbing it down or over-explaining.

**2. Premium-Understated**  
We don't shout. We let credentials speak. Roland Garros and FUTURIUM are stated as facts, not boasts.

**3. Direct-American**  
European precision does not mean European verbosity. We are clear, short, action-oriented. American clients value time.

**4. Trustworthy-Transparent**  
25 years. Real numbers. Real clients. No vague claims — every statement is backed by a proof point.

## 3. WORDS TO USE / WORDS TO AVOID

### Use These Words

**Technical:**
- engineered, precision, fabricated, specified, certified
- tempered, laminated, insulated, coated, curved, bent
- glazing, facade, curtain wall, structural glass
- low-E, solar control, thermal performance, acoustic
- antimicrobial, hygienic, sterile-grade
- LEED-compatible, energy-efficient, sustainable

**Premium/Positioning:**
- European-engineered, crafted, bespoke, custom
- landmark, signature, award-winning
- proven, trusted, certified, compliant
- precision, accuracy, quality, performance

**American-Speed/Directness:**
- delivered, on-time, 4–6 weeks, ready, available
- direct, one contact, no middlemen
- quote, specs, drawings, proposal

---

### Avoid These Words

**Avoid (overused/weak):**
- "solutions" used generically (say specifically what you offer)
- "world-class" (unsubstantiated)
- "cutting-edge" / "state-of-the-art" (cliché)
- "we are proud to..." (passive, sales-ish)
- "innovative" without specifics (empty)
- "affordable" / "cheap" (wrong brand position)
- "fast delivery" without the number (say "4–6 weeks")

**Avoid (wrong register):**
- Exclamation points in professional copy
- First person plural "We are excited!" enthusiasm
- Jargon that only Europeans use without explanation
- Transliterated Romanian expressions

## 6. VISUAL BRAND LANGUAGE (Copy Guidance)

### For Photography/Image Captions
- Always name the project and location
- Always name the product used
- Format: "[Product] · [Project Name] · [City, Country]"
- Example: "EVOPRINT Ceramic Glass · FUTURIUM · Berlin, Germany"

### For Case Study Headlines
- Format: "[Challenge] → [Solution] → [Outcome]"
- Example: "Complex curved facade → Custom bent glass EVOLAM → Installed on-schedule at Roland Garros"

### For Testimonial Framing
- Lead with the role, then the company, then the quote
- Example: "Project Architect, SKANSKA USA: '...'"
- Never use first names only

## 7. US LOCALIZATION RULES

### Language Differences (EN-US not EN-GB/EN-EU)

| EU/UK English | US English |
|---------------|------------|
| colour | color |
| specialise | specialize |
| aluminium | aluminum |
| façade (with cedilla) | facade (acceptable without in US) |
| glazier | glazing contractor |
| metres / millimetres | feet/inches (or dual: mm / inches) |
| turnover | revenue |
| bespoke | custom |

### Units — Dual Format for Specifications
When listing technical specs, always provide US measurements first:
- "1/4" (6mm) tempered glass"
- "1" (25mm) insulated glass unit"

### Legal/Compliance References
- Always cite EN standards AND note "ASTM-compatible documentation available"
- CE certification should be mentioned alongside UL/ANSI where relevant
- Do not make compliance claims that are not certified
