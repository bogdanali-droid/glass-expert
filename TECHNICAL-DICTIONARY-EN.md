## GLASS TYPES & PRODUCTS

| English Term | Romanian | Definition | Applications |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Annealed Glass** | Sticlă liniară/răcită lent | Standard float glass cooled slowly; weakest type; breaks into large shards | Interior windows, low-safety areas |
| **Tempered Glass** | Sticlă temperată | Heat-treated glass (1200°C); 4-5x stronger; breaks into safe cubes (not shards); ASTM C1048 | Doors, facades, high-traffic areas |
| **Laminated Glass** | Sticlă laminată | Multiple glass layers bonded with PVB (polyvinyl butyral) interlayer; stays intact when broken | Safety applications, soundproofing, security |
| **Curved Glass** | Sticlă curbă | Custom-bent glass with specific radius; architectural feature; requires specialty manufacturing | Facades, interior design features |
| **Ceramic-Printed Glass** | Sticlă cu tipărire ceramică | Screen-printed enamel fused at 700-800°C; permanent; customizable colors/patterns | Branding, decorative, commercial interior |
| **Laminated Tempered** | Sticlă laminată temperată | Tempered glass with lamination; maximum strength + safety | High-spec commercial, healthcare |
| **Low-E Glass** | Sticlă cu strat Low-E | Coated with metallic oxide; reduces heat transfer; thermal efficiency | Facade, energy efficiency (IECC 2024) |
| **Smart Glass** | Sticlă inteligentă (EVOLAM) | Electrochromic coating; transparency controlled electronically; privacy on-demand | Luxury hospitality, offices, research |
| **Acoustic Glass** | Sticlă acustică | Laminated with special PVB; dampens sound; STC rating 35-45+dB | Offices, hospitality, noise isolation |
| **Anti-Microbial Glass** | Sticlă anti-microbiene | Integrated antimicrobial coating; resists bacterial colonization; medical-grade | Healthcare, cleanrooms, surgical suites |
| **Chemical-Resistant Glass** | Sticlă rezistentă chimie | Coating resists caustic materials/acids/bases | Research labs, chemical facilities |
| **Cleanroom Glass** | Sticlă cleanroom | Laminated, zero particle shedding, meets USP <825> standard | Medical cleanrooms, pharmaceutical labs |
| **Tinted Glass** | Sticlă colorată | Colored glass; reduces glare/heat; aesthetic | Commercial facades, privacy |
| **Frosted Glass** | Sticlă translucidă | Sand-blasted or etched surface; diffuses light; opaque privacy | Interior partitions, bathrooms, offices |

## GLASS SPECIFICATIONS & PERFORMANCE

| English Term | Romanian | Definition | USA Standard |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Thickness** | Grosime | Glass thickness in mm (3mm, 4mm, 6mm, 8mm, 10mm, 12mm) | ASTM C1036 |
| **U-Value** | Factor U | Thermal transmittance (lower = more insulation); measured W/m²K | IECC 2024 |
| **SHGC** | SHGC (coeff. guain solar) | Solar Heat Gain Coefficient; 0-1 scale; lower = less heat | IECC 2024 |
| **VLT (Visible Light Transmittance)** | VLT (Transmisia luminii vizibile) | Percentage of visible light passing through (0-100%); higher = clearer | ASTM E1300 |
| **STC (Sound Transmission Class)** | STC (Clasa transmisie sunet) | Acoustic rating 0-80; higher = better soundproofing; typical 28-45 | ASTM C423 |
| **Thermal Stress** | Stres termic | Glass fracture from thermal gradient; prevented by Low-E, proper installation | ASTM C1036 |
| **Wind Load Rating** | Rating vânt | Resistance to pressure; specified as PSF (pounds per square foot) | ASTM E1886 |
| **Impact Resistance** | Rezistență impact | Glass durability under physical impact; tempered resists more | ASTM C1172 |
| **Haze** | Turbiditate | Light scatter (%); lower = clearer; important for curved glass quality | ASTM E1003 |
| **Color Consistency** | Consistență culoare | Uniformity of tint/coating across glass; critical for architectural specs | Visual inspection |

## USAGE GUIDE

**When writing website copy, use terms from this dictionary for:**

1. **Accuracy** — Use proper technical terms (tempered vs. annealed, not "strong glass")
2. **Credibility** — Shows engineering/technical knowledge to architects/engineers
3. **SEO** — Technical terms improve search ranking with B2B buyers
4. **Compliance** — Standards references (ASTM, EN, IBC) reassure facility managers
5. **Differentiation** — Showing superior specs (99.2% first-pass quality, 4-6 week lead time) vs. competitors

**Example:**

❌ *Wrong:* "We make strong glass that's safe."
✅ *Right:* "ASTM C1172-certified laminated tempered glass with 99.2% first-pass quality, 15-year hermetic seal warranty."
