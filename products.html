EVOPRINT Family

Digital ceramic printing technology fused directly into glass for permanent, high-resolution architectural graphics and colors.

EVOPRINT

EVOPRINT

Ceramic-Printed Glass

Advanced digital printing technology that applies ceramic inks directly onto glass and fires them at high temperatures. The result is a permanent, UV-stable, scratch-resistant image that becomes part of the glass itself — not a film or coating.

  • Architectural facades and curtain walls
  • Interior partitions and feature walls
  • Branded corporate spaces
  • Decorative panels and art glass
  • Spandrel glass and sun shading

EVOLAM Family

Multi-layer laminated glass systems — from standard safety glass to curved architectural masterpieces and smart switchable glass.

EVOLAM

EVOLAM

Laminated Safety Glass

Two or more glass layers bonded with a PVB or EVA interlayer. When broken, fragments are held in place by the interlayer, drastically reducing injury risk. Meets the highest safety standards for public and high-traffic environments.

  • Overhead glazing and skylights
  • Facades and curtain walls
  • Shopfronts and public spaces
  • Balustrades and staircases
  • Acoustic barriers
EVOLAM T

EVOLAM T

Tempered Laminated Glass

Combines the strength of tempered glass with the safety of lamination. Each layer is thermally tempered before lamination, delivering superior impact resistance while maintaining the structural integrity of laminated glass.

  • High-performance facades
  • Security glazing
  • Hurricane and storm protection
  • Floors and walkways
  • Structural glazing applications
EVOLAM K

EVOLAM K

Colored Film Laminated Glass

Laminated glass with colored PVB interlayers that create vibrant, translucent color effects. The color is embedded within the glass structure — it cannot fade, peel, or scratch. Available in a full spectrum of colors and transparencies.

  • Designer partitions and feature walls
  • Colored facades and canopies
  • Hospitality and retail interiors
  • Branded architectural elements
  • Artistic installations
EVOLAM DESIGN

EVOLAM DESIGN

Insert Laminated Glass

Unique decorative elements — fabrics, metals, botanicals, woven materials, or custom inserts — are encapsulated between glass layers. Creates one-of-a-kind glass panels that function as architectural art.

  • Luxury hotel lobbies and suites
  • High-end retail and showrooms
  • Corporate headquarters
  • Bespoke residential projects
  • Museum and gallery installations
EVOLAM CONTOUR

EVOLAM CONTOUR

Curved Laminated Glass

Laminated glass bent to custom curves and contours. Both layers are formed simultaneously, ensuring perfect alignment and optical clarity. Enables dramatic architectural shapes without compromising safety performance.

  • Curved facades and bay windows
  • Architectural canopies and atriums
  • Cylindrical and freeform structures
  • Luxury interiors with curved walls
  • Custom bespoke architectural projects
Most Innovative
EVOLAM SMART

EVOLAM SMART

Smart Switchable Glass

Revolutionary PDLC (Polymer Dispersed Liquid Crystal) technology laminated between glass layers. A simple electrical signal instantly switches the glass from fully transparent to fully opaque — on demand, without blinds or curtains. Energy efficient, architecturally pure.

  • Corporate boardrooms and meeting rooms
  • Hospital patient privacy
  • Hotel spa and bathroom glass
  • Retail window displays
  • Smart home privacy glass

EVODUR Family

Thermally processed glass with exceptional strength — from full tempered safety glass to semi-tempered structural glazing.

EVODUR ESG

EVODUR ESG

Tempered Safety Glass

Float glass heated to 650°C then rapidly quenched creates compressive stresses that give tempered glass 4-5x the mechanical strength of standard glass. When broken, it shatters into small, blunt fragments — dramatically reducing injury risk. Compliant with EN 12150 and ASTM C1048.

  • Facades, curtain walls, and shopfronts
  • Internal partitions and doors
  • Balustrades and railings
  • Furniture glass (tabletops, shelves)
  • Automotive and transportation
EVODUR TVG

EVODUR TVG

Semi-Tempered Glass (Heat Strengthened)

Heat-strengthened glass processed at lower temperatures than full tempering. Provides approximately twice the strength of annealed glass while breaking into larger fragments — ideal when fragments must remain in place after breakage. Required for structural glazing and laminated applications.

  • Structural glazing and glass fins
  • Overhead glazing substrates
  • Lamination base for facades
  • Large-format architectural panels
  • Point-fixed glazing systems

EVOKERAM Family

Ceramic enamel applied and fired onto glass — permanent color and opacity for architectural cladding and spandrel applications.

EVOKERAM R

EVOKERAM R

Ceramic Enameled Glass — Roller Applied

Ceramic enamel applied via precision rollers for perfectly uniform opaque coverage, then fired to create a permanent bond with the glass surface. Ideal for large spandrel panels requiring consistent color across an entire facade.

  • Spandrel panels in curtain wall systems
  • Facade cladding and rainscreen
  • Opaque architectural glazing
  • Solar control facades
  • Interior wall cladding
EVOKERAM S

EVOKERAM S

Ceramic Screen-Printed Glass

Ceramic paste applied through precision silk-screen printing, enabling complex patterns, gradients, and designs to be permanently fused onto glass. Combines decorative flexibility with the durability of ceramic enamel — patterns rated for 25+ years of exterior exposure.

  • Patterned facade elements
  • Gradient frit and shading patterns
  • Decorative architectural glass
  • Privacy screens with design patterns
  • Custom branding and wayfinding

Functional Performance Glass

Specialized coatings and treatments that solve real-world problems — from furniture scratch resistance to self-cleaning exteriors and smart electrical transmission.

EVOTOP

EVOTOP

Anti-Scratch Glass

Specialized hardness coating applied to glass surfaces used in furniture and high-contact applications. Dramatically increases surface resistance to scratches, abrasion, and everyday wear — extending the aesthetic life of glass furniture and table tops without compromising clarity or touch.

  • Glass table tops and desks
  • Furniture shelving and cabinets
  • Retail display cases
  • High-traffic countertops
  • Luxury residential and hotel furniture
EVOCLEAN

EVOCLEAN

Self-Cleaning Glass

Photocatalytic and hydrophilic coating that uses UV light to break down organic dirt, then allows rain water to sheet off and carry particles away — leaving glass clean. The anti-calcification properties prevent mineral deposits, dramatically reducing maintenance costs for exterior glazing.

  • High-rise facade glass (hard to clean)
  • Skylights and canopies
  • Solar panels protective glass
  • Greenhouse glazing
  • Sustainable building certification projects
EVOTECH

EVOTECH

Industrial / Electrical Command Glass

Conductive coatings applied to glass enable electrical current transmission directly through the glass surface. Enables glass to function as a heating element, antenna, touch interface, or part of a smart building control system — without visible wiring.

  • Heated glass (anti-frost, anti-condensation)
  • Touch-sensitive glass interfaces
  • Smart building energy management
  • Industrial process glazing
  • Integrated antenna systems
