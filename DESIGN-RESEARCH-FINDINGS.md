## 🔬 RESEARCH FINDINGS (2024-2025 USA Market)

### Futuristic Trends That Matter
Based on research of top architectural glass design trends:

**1. Smart Glass / Electrochromic Technology** ⚡
- Dynamic transparency control (clear → opaque at touch)
- High-tech, futuristic positioning
- Appeals to: Tech-forward architects, corporate HQs
- **Use Case:** Headline feature for premium positioning

**2. Fluted & Textured Glass** 🌊
- Trending HARD in 2024-2025
- Soft vertical lines, beautiful light diffusion
- Premium aesthetic with subtle privacy
- **Use Case:** Design feature for luxury hospitality/interiors

**3. Frameless & Minimalist Design** ✨
- Clean lines, contemporary aesthetic
- High-rise offices using frameless curtain walls
- "Less is more" philosophy
- **Use Case:** Commercial facade messaging

**4. Energy Efficiency Focus** 🌱
- Low-E coatings, solar control
- LEED certifications, green building
- Sustainability = luxury now
- **Use Case:** Corporate responsibility angle

**5. Bold Color Statements** 🎭
- Jewel tones in hospitality (emerald, sapphire, ruby)
- Colored glass as design feature
- **Use Case:** Design partnership angle

**6. Sustainable Materials** ♻️
- Recycled glass positioning
- Environmental impact messaging
- **Use Case:** Corporate/institutional appeal

**Hero Aesthetic:**
- Dark, sophisticated background (charcoal #2C2C2C or deep navy)

- Headlines: Bold, geometric (Poppins Extrabold or Montserrat Bold)

**Color Palette (Premium):**
