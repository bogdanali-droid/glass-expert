## 📊 MARKET RESEARCH — USA ARCHITECTURAL GLASS WEBSITES

### COMPETITIVE DESIGN ANALYSIS

#### 1. **SAINT-GOBAIN (sg-building-products.com)**
- **Design approach:** Corporate, enterprise-heavy
- **Color scheme:** Gray + blue + white (trust, authority)
- **Typography:** Sans-serif, hierarchical
- **User experience:** Complex navigation, lots of product categories
- **Weakness:** Feels corporate, not innovative; overwhelming for first-time visitors
- **Strength:** Professional credibility

#### 2. **GUARDIAN INDUSTRIES (guardianproducts.com)**
- **Design approach:** Modern, clean, manufacturing-focused
- **Color scheme:** Teal + white + dark blue
- **Typography:** Geometric sans-serif
- **User experience:** Product-centric, specs/data-heavy
- **Weakness:** Technical, boring; no emotional connection
- **Strength:** Clear information hierarchy

#### 3. **PILKINGTON (pilkington.com)**
- **Design approach:** Premium positioning, minimalist
- **Color scheme:** Soft grays + accent green (sustainability)
- **Typography:** Premium serif + modern sans-serif mix
- **User experience:** Storytelling approach, innovation focus
- **Weakness:** Slow navigation, outdated mobile
- **Strength:** Premium brand perception

#### 4. **VIRACON (viracon.com)**
- **Design approach:** Modern, performance-focused
- **Color scheme:** Deep blue + white + gold accents
- **Typography:** Bold sans-serif headers
- **User experience:** Visual case studies, interactive demos
- **Weakness:** Regional (USA), not global
- **Strength:** High-tech, modern aesthetic

#### 5. **PPG GLASS (ppg.com/en/us)**
- **Design approach:** Corporate but trendy
- **Color scheme:** Yellow + blue + white (brand recognition)
- **Typography:** Bold sans-serif, modern
- **User experience:** Segment-focused navigation (same as our strategy!)
- **Weakness:** Heavy on corporate messaging
- **Strength:** Clear segment navigation mirrors our market segmentation

## 🔍 CURRENT XGLASS.EU DESIGN ANALYSIS

**Website:** xglass.eu (European market positioning)

### Design Elements:
- **Color scheme:** Dark navy + metallic accents + white
- **Typography:** Modern sans-serif (clean, professional)
- **Imagery:** Manufacturing, product close-ups, buildings
- **User flow:** Company → Products → Services → Contact
- **Aesthetic:** European industrial (premium but serious)
- **Strength:** Shows manufacturing expertise, quality
- **Weakness:** Not optimized for US market; feels "European corporate"

### What to **KEEP** from xglass.eu:
✅ Premium positioning (not cheap)
✅ Manufacturing credibility (show factory)
✅ Quality focus (certifications, testing)
✅ European elegance (refinement, not flashiness)

### What to **EVOLVE** for USA market:
❌ Seriousness → Add innovation narrative
❌ Europe-only → Global, USA-centric
❌ Products → Solutions (segment-focused)
❌ Static → Interactive, visual storytelling

## 🎨 DESIGN DIRECTION — 4 MARKET SEGMENTS

### COLOR STRATEGY (Primary Brand Colors)

**Main Brand:** 
- Deep Blue: `#1B3A6B` (trust, innovation, European heritage)
- Accent Gold: `#D4AF37` (premium, quality, distinction)
- Clean White: `#FFFFFF` (space, clarity, modern)

**Segment-Specific Accents:**
- **Commercial Real Estate:** Teal `#0E7C86` (modern, architectural)
- **Hospitality:** Warm Copper `#B87333` (luxury, welcoming)
- **Healthcare:** Mint Green `#6FCF97` (trust, healing, clean)
- **Education:** Deep Purple `#4B0082` (innovation, learning)

### TYPOGRAPHY STRATEGY
- **Headlines:** "Poppins Bold" or "Inter Bold" (geometric, modern, premium)
- **Body:** "Inter Regular" or "Segoe UI" (clean, readable, professional)
- **Accent/Quotes:** "Georgia" or "Crimson Text" (sophisticated, European)

### IMAGERY DIRECTION
- **Hero sections:** Curved glass in architectural context (not just products)
- **Segment pages:** Beautiful building interiors (Commercial), luxury hotels (Hospitality), clean medical spaces (Healthcare), modern universities (Education)
- **Process:** Show fabrication (factory, CNC machines) but beautiful, artistic photography
- **People:** Architects, designers, facility managers (aspirational, professional)
- **Avoid:** Clichés (stock photos), overly polished (feel fake), dark moody (feels heavy)

## 🎨 VISUAL DESIGN SYSTEM

### Homepage Hero Section
**Current:** Static gradient background
**Redesign:** 
- Video background (beautiful curved glass catching light) OR animated glass manufacturing
- Overlay: Semi-transparent dark blue
- Text: Large, bold, aspirational
- CTA Buttons: Gold accent, hover animation (subtle glow)

### Segment Pages Layout
**Pattern:** Alternating image/text blocks

```
Block 1: Problem Statement (Text left, icon right)
Block 2: Solution (Image left, text right)
Block 3: Technical Specs (Dark background, white text, icons)
Block 4: Case Study (Full-width image + overlay with story)
Block 5: CTA Section (Color-coded per segment)
```

### Card Component Design
```
[Image (hero shot of glass in context)]
[Headline: Bold, color-coded per segment]
[Description: 2-3 sentences]
[Icon representing feature]
[CTA: Subtle, color-coded]
```

### Typography Hierarchy
```
H1: 3.5rem, Bold (hero headlines)
H2: 2.5rem, Bold (section headlines)
H3: 1.8rem, Semi-bold (subsections)
Body: 1rem, Regular (readable, 1.6 line-height)
Small: 0.875rem, Regular (captions, footnotes)
```

## 📱 MOBILE-FIRST DESIGN

### Mobile Priorities:
1. **Fast load** (architects on-site, poor WiFi)
   - Image optimization (WebP, lazy load)
   - Minimal animations (mobile CPUs)
   - Readable text (16px+ font sizes)

2. **Easy navigation** (one-handed use)
   - Bottom navigation (easier to reach)
   - Large touch targets (48px minimum)
   - Reduced menu depth

3. **Form optimization**
   - Mobile-friendly forms (segmented)
   - Auto-fill enabled
   - Single-column layout
   - Large input fields

4. **Speed** (PageSpeed >90)
   - Optimize images
   - Minify CSS/JS
   - Enable caching
   - CDN delivery
