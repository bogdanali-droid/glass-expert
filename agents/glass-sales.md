## Outreach Strategy

### 1. Cold Email Sequence

**Target:** Architecture firms + contractors working on Commercial Real Estate, Hospitality, Healthcare projects

**Database Source:**
- MARKET-SEGMENTATION-LEADS-30K.md (pre-research)
- LinkedIn search (architecture + USA)
- AIA directory (American Institute of Architects)
- AGC directory (Associated General Contractors)

**Email Template (Segment-Specific):**

```
Subject: Curved Glass for [Project Type] — 4-6 Week Delivery

Hi [Name],

We're seeing architects in [City] specify curved glass facades for their flagship projects.

Problem we solve: Local glass suppliers take 8-12 weeks. Your timeline likely requires 6 weeks max.

What we do:
- In-house curved glass manufacturing (rare in USA)
- 4-6 week delivery (vs competitor standard 8-12)
- ASTM + EN certifications (European + USA standards)
- Full design consultation included

Example: [Recent project case study if available]

Would a 15-min call make sense to see if we're a fit?

Best,
[Your name]
Glass Expert USA
```

**Customization by Segment:**
- **Commercial:** Focus on speed, curves, facade complexity
- **Hospitality:** Focus on design partnership, ceramic-printed, customization
- **Healthcare:** Focus on medical-grade, cleanroom, FDA compliance
- **Education:** Focus on durability, sustainability, bulk pricing

**Campaign Structure:**
- Week 1: 50 personalized emails (1st touch)
- Week 2: 40 follow-up emails (2nd touch, different angle)
- Week 3: 30 re-engagement emails (3rd touch, specific case study)
- Ongoing: 20 emails/week (maintain pipeline)

## Lead Qualification (BANT)

**Criteria for "Qualified Lead":**

✅ **Budget** — Estimated project value >$50K (glass portion)  
✅ **Authority** — Contact is decision-maker or influencer (architect, PM, procurement)  
✅ **Need** — Project requires: curved glass OR custom glass OR fast delivery  
✅ **Timeline** — Project timeline 3-12 months from now  

**Disqualify if:**
- [ ] Budget <$50K
- [ ] Contact not in decision chain
- [ ] Project uses standard commodity glass only
- [ ] Timeline >18 months out
- [ ] Company outside our geographic focus

## Lead Tracking & Pipeline Management

**Track in CRM (via @glass-crm):**
1. **New** — Email sent, awaiting response
2. **Interested** — Response received, conversation started
3. **Qualified** — BANT criteria met, scheduled call
4. **Proposal** — Quote sent, awaiting decision
5. **Negotiation** — Discussing terms, moving to close
6. **Won** — Deal closed, project begins
7. **Lost** — Lost reason tracked for learning

**Metrics to Track:**
- Emails sent/day
- Response rate (%)
- Leads per segment
- Lead source (bid board vs cold email)
- Days from lead → proposal
- Proposal → close conversion (%)
- Pipeline value (€)

**Daily Reporting:** Submit standup with metrics to `shared/memory/daily-standups.json`
