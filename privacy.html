Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

1. Introduction

Glass Expert USA ("we", "us", "our") and VAGOTECH operate glassexpertusa.com and related services. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our website.

2. Information We Collect

Contact Forms

When you submit a contact form, we collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Company/Organization
  • Project description or inquiry
  • Budget range

Automatic Information (Analytics Tracking)

When you visit our site, we may collect:

  • Device type and browser
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Referral source
  • Visitor ID (stored in localStorage)
  • Product interactions (clicks, selections)

3. GDPR & Consent

Before we collect any analytics data, you will see a consent banner asking for your permission. You can:

  • Accept — Allow analytics tracking
  • Decline — Opt out of all tracking (contact forms still work)
  • Manage — Delete your visitor data at any time

Consent is required before tracking begins. We do not track without your explicit permission.

4. Data Retention

Policy: No automatic deletion. We retain visitor analytics data indefinitely unless you request deletion.

Contact form submissions are retained for business purposes and may be archived for 7 years for compliance and record-keeping.

5. How We Use Your Data

  • Contact Forms: To respond to your inquiry and provide product information
  • Analytics: To improve website experience, understand visitor behavior, optimize product pages
  • Compliance: To comply with legal obligations

We do NOT sell or share your data with third parties.

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Deletion: Request we delete your visitor profile and analytics data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a standard format
  • Withdraw Consent: Stop tracking at any time

To exercise these rights, email: office.us@xglass.eu

7. Data Security

We use HTTPS encryption for all data transmission. Analytics data is stored in Cloudflare D1 (SQLite database) with access restricted to authorized personnel only.

8. Cookies & Local Storage

We use:

  • localStorage: To store your visitor_id (for analytics tracking)
  • cookies: None (we do not use cookies)

You can clear localStorage at any time via browser settings. This will reset your visitor profile.

9. Third-Party Services

We use:

  • Cloudflare Pages: For website hosting
  • Feather Icons (CDN): For icon display

These services may collect limited technical data (IP address, page load times). Refer to their privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be announced on this page. Continued use of our site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
