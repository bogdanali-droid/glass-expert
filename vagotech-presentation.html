Compliance & Certification

The Four Standards That Define Railway Glass Procurement

VAGOTECH glass meets all four. Documented compliance delivered with every order.

EN 15152

Glass for driver-cab windscreens on rail vehicles — impact resistance, optical clarity, structural integrity requirements for front glazing on locomotives, EMUs and trams.

EN 15153

Glass for side and door windows on rail vehicles — covers tempered and laminated options, dimensional tolerances, thermal and mechanical performance for passenger glazing.

EN 12600

Pendulum impact test and safety classification for flat glass — defines impact behaviour categories (1B1 through 3C3) required across all rail vehicle interior and exterior glazing applications.

⚠ EU Tendering Prerequisite
EN 45545

Fire behaviour of materials and components — mandatory for any glass product supplied under EU rail tenders issued after 2023. Covers fire reaction, smoke toxicity, and heat release. Suppliers without this certification cannot win public procurement contracts.
