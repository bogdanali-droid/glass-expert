## 📋 Brand Overview

**Glas Expert USA** is the American market entry for Glas Expert Europe—a premium architectural glass supplier targeting €30K+ commercial projects. The brand positions cutting-edge glass engineering as the foundation of iconic architecture.

### Brand Promise
"European precision. American speed. Architectural impact."

### Core Values
- **Innovation** — Cutting-edge glass technology (smart glass, ceramic-printed, curved)
- **Precision** — 24 years European manufacturing expertise
- **Speed** — 4-6 week delivery vs 8-12 weeks competitors
- **Sustainability** — LEED-certified, recycled materials, low-E thermal

### DO's & DON'Ts

✅ **DO:**
- Use dark backgrounds (#1B1B1B, #1B3A6B) for premium hero sections
- Apply bright accents (#00D4FF, #00C884) to interactive elements
- Leverage segment colors for clear market positioning
- Maintain high contrast for accessibility
- Use white/light backgrounds for readability sections

❌ **DON'T:**
- Mix Romania branding colors (too conservative for USA market)
- Use more than 2 colors per section (visual clarity)
- Apply bright accents to body text (reserved for CTAs/interaction)
- Create gradients that reduce readability

## 🔤 TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM

### Font Stack

**Headlines (H1-H3):** Poppins Bold / Extrabold
- Weight: 700-800
- Letter Spacing: 1-2px (for premium feel)
- Size Range: 2.5rem (H1) → 1.5rem (H3)
- Style: All-caps for tech terminology (VERSATIKA, SMART GLASS, LEED)

**Subheadings (H4-H5):** Inter SemiBold
- Weight: 600
- Size: 1.1rem → 0.95rem
- Color: Primary or segment color

**Body Text:** Inter Regular
- Weight: 400
- Line Height: 1.6-1.8
- Color: #1B3A6B or #666666
- Size: 0.95-1rem

**CTA Text:** Poppins Bold
- Weight: 700
- All-caps: "SCHEDULE CONSULTATION" "GET QUOTE" "DESIGN YOUR SPACE"

### Typography Rules

✅ **Premium Voice:**
- Declarative headlines: "Premium Glass for American Architecture"
- Active verbs in CTAs: "Schedule," "Design," "Build," "Shape"
- Avoid corporate jargon; use accessible language
- Tone: Ambitious but approachable

Example Headlines:
- "Curved Glass. No Compromises."
- "Sustainable Glass, Healing Spaces"
- "Innovation in Every Classroom"
- "Bespoke Glass as Your Design Signature"

## 📐 VISUAL DESIGN ELEMENTS

### Backgrounds & Gradients

**Hero Sections:**
```css
background: linear-gradient(135deg, [dark-segment-color] 0%, [bright-segment-color] 100%);
```

Example:
- Commercial: `#0066AA` → `#00D4FF`
- Hospitality: `#CC5220` → `#FF6B35`
- Healthcare: `#007755` → `#00C884`
- Education: `#4B0082` → `#6C5CE7`

**Product Cards:**
- White background with colored left/top border (2-3px segment color)
- Subtle shadow on hover
- Rounded corners (8-10px)
- Smooth color transitions

**Section Dividers:**
- Light gray (#F9F9F9) alternating sections
- No harsh borders; rely on whitespace

### Imagery Style

**Hero Images:**
- Modern office/architectural photography
- Glass prominently featured
- High contrast lighting (dramatic, tech-forward)
- Bright, optimistic color palette
- 16:9 aspect ratio minimum

**Product Photography:**
- Clean, white background OR architectural context
- Close-ups showing texture/detail
- Professional studio lighting
- Product in real-world application (optional)

**Case Study Imagery:**
- Complete architectural shots
- Building exteriors/interiors showing glass solution
- Aspirational, premium aesthetic
- High resolution (3000+ px width)

**Icons & Graphics:**
- Simple, geometric style
- 2-3 colors maximum per icon
- Consistent stroke weight
- Emoji as fallback for quick mockups

### Layout Patterns

**Section Structure:**
1. Hero (Full-width, gradient, typography-focused)
2. Value proposition (3-column grid, icons + text)
3. Products/Solutions (Auto-fit grid, min 280px cards)
4. Case study (Image + narrative, alternating layout)
5. CTA (Full-width, high contrast color)
6. Form (Max 600px center-aligned, clear labels)

**Whitespace:**
- 4rem padding top/bottom sections (mobile: 2rem)
- 2rem gap between grid items
- 1.5rem internal card padding

## 📊 DESIGN SPECIFICATIONS

### Button Styles

**Primary CTA:**
- Background: Segment color or #00D4FF (bright accent)
- Text: White or very dark
- Padding: 1rem 2rem
- Border radius: 5-8px
- Font weight: 700
- Hover: Darken by 10% or add shadow

**Secondary CTA:**
- Background: Transparent or light gray
- Border: 2px segment color
- Text: Segment color
- Padding: 0.75rem 1.5rem

### Form Elements

**Input Fields:**
- Border: 1px #E0E0E0
- Background: #FFFFFF
- Padding: 0.75rem 1rem
- Border radius: 5px
- Focus: Border color = segment color, no outline

**Labels:**
- Font: Inter 500, 0.95rem
- Color: #1B3A6B
- Margin bottom: 0.5rem
