Railway Glass Product Lines

Windshield Glass

Laminated safety glass for locomotive and train car windshields. Impact resistant, optical clarity, UV protection.

  • Standard: EN 12288 (railway vehicles)
  • Type: Laminated tempered
  • Acoustic: Noise reduction option
  • Lead Time: Custom 6-8 weeks

Side Panel Glass

Passenger side windows and door glass. Tempered for safety, laminated options for acoustic and thermal performance.

  • Standard: EN 14752 (railway safety)
  • Type: Tempered or laminated
  • Sizes: Custom dimensions
  • Lead Time: 6-8 weeks

Interior Panels

Cabin partition glass, crew area partitions, emergency window panels. Durable, lightweight, scratch-resistant coatings.

  • Standard: EN 12600 (impact testing)
  • Finishes: Clear, frosted, tinted
  • Coating: Anti-scratch, easy-clean options
  • Lead Time: 6-8 weeks

Custom Solutions

Specialized glass for doors, roof panels, observation windows. Engineered to your exact specifications and budget.

  • Engineering: Full design consultation
  • Materials: Tempered, laminated, curved, coated
  • Testing: Thermal, impact, acoustic certification
