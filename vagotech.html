The VAGOTECH Story

European-Engineered Glass for a Railway Network in Full Modernization

VAGOTECH is the railway and tramway division of Glas Expert — a European architectural glass manufacturer with 25+ years of experience and a place among the Top 20 glass processors in Europe. The same precision engineering proven on landmark projects across the continent is now focused on a single mission: supplying rail-grade glass to Romania's modernizing fleet operators.

Why now. Romania's rail network is in the middle of its largest modernization in decades. CFR Călători is introducing new Alstom Coradia Stream electric trainsets and Traxx locomotives. Bucharest's STB has deployed 100 Astra Imperio trams and has contracted dozens more. New rolling stock and refurbishment programs all share one requirement: certified, dimensionally exact glass delivered on a dependable schedule.

The challenge operators face. Rail glass must survive thermal cycling, vibration, impact and decades of daily duty — while meeting non-negotiable European standards (EN 15152 for windscreens, EN 15153/EN 12600 for impact). A single specification mismatch or a slipped delivery can hold an entire vehicle in the workshop. Operators need a processor that treats glass as a precision component, not a commodity.

The VAGOTECH advantage. In-house tempering, lamination, curved shaping and custom edge work mean no outsourcing and no compromises. We engineer to exact OEM and operator specifications, document full compliance, and deliver on a schedule fleet planners can build around. European quality, Romanian proximity, single-supplier accountability.

Understanding the Romanian Rail Market Opportunity

CFR Fleet Renewal

CFR Călători is introducing new Alstom Coradia Stream EMUs (up to 160 km/h, EU-funded) and 16 Traxx locomotives under a €150M programme. New trainsets and parallel coach refurbishment create a sustained demand for certified rail glazing.

STB Tram Expansion

Bucharest's STB has put 100 Astra Imperio trams into service and contracted financing for 63 new 36-metre trams plus 16 for the Prelungirea Ghencea line. Each vehicle — new or refurbished — needs side, door and windscreen glass.

Domestic OEM Supply

Romanian builders such as Electroputere VFU Pașcani and Astra Vagoane Arad assemble rolling stock for domestic and regional markets. OEMs need a glass partner with European certifications and reliable lead times. VAGOTECH is built for that role.

VAGOTECH Product Solutions

Purpose-built glass for every railway application. Engineered. Tested. Certified.

Windscreen Glass

The challenge: Driver cab windscreens on locomotives and EMUs need crystal clarity, must withstand temperature extremes, resist high-energy impacts from ballast and debris, and hold structural integrity across decades of service.

VAGOTECH solution: Laminated, heat-strengthened glass engineered to EN 15152. Custom curvature to OEM cab geometry. Optional heating layer for de-icing, UV-protective and acoustic interlayers.

  • Standard: EN 15152 (rail vehicle windscreens)
  • Type: Laminated + heat-strengthened
  • Options: Heated / de-icing layer
  • Impact: High-energy ballast impact resistant
  • Acoustic: Noise-reduction interlayer
  • Lead Time: 6–8 weeks custom

Passenger Side & Door Glass

The challenge: Passenger and door windows face constant thermal cycling and vibration while carrying comfort and safety expectations — clarity, noise reduction, and certified impact behaviour. STB trams and CFR coaches run these windows for decades.

VAGOTECH solution: Tempered or laminated glazing to EN 15152/EN 15153 and EN 12600 impact class. Custom dimensions, curves and angles for any car. Acoustic interlayers and easy-clean coatings on request.

  • Standard: EN 15153 / EN 12600 (impact)
  • Type: Tempered or laminated
  • Dimensions: Custom curves and angles
  • Acoustic: Noise-reduction interlayer
  • Coatings: Easy-clean / low-maintenance
  • Lead Time: 6–8 weeks

Interior & Partition Glass

The challenge: Interior partitions, crew screens and emergency windows need certified impact resistance, durability and low maintenance. Crew and passenger safety is paramount, and modern interiors demand a clean, transparent feel.

VAGOTECH solution: Tempered or laminated interior glass to EN 12600 impact classification. Clear, frosted or tinted finishes. Anti-scratch and easy-clean coatings. Lightweight builds support vehicle energy efficiency.

  • Standard: EN 12600 (impact classification)
  • Finishes: Clear, frosted, tinted
  • Impact: Classified safety behaviour
  • Coatings: Anti-scratch, easy-clean
  • Weight: Optimised for vehicle efficiency
  • Lead Time: 6–8 weeks

Custom Rail Solutions

The challenge: Specialised applications — roof glazing, observation windows, emergency exits, destination-blind glass — each with unique geometry and OEM specifications, often under tight refurbishment budgets and timelines.

VAGOTECH solution: Full engineering consultation from drawing to delivery. Any shape, size or build. Documented testing and compliance. On-time delivery with dedicated project management for fleet-scale orders.

  • Engineering: Design to OEM / operator specs
  • Builds: Tempered, laminated, curved, coated
  • Compliance: Documented EN testing
  • Expertise: 25+ years glass engineering
  • Capacity: Fleet-scale orders accommodated
  • Lead Time: 8–12 weeks custom
