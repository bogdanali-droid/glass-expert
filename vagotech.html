VAGOTECH Product Solutions

Purpose-built glass for every railway application. Engineered. Tested. Certified.

Windshield Glass

The Challenge: Locomotive and train car drivers need windshields that provide crystal clarity, withstand temperature extremes (-30°C to +70°C), resist impacts from flying debris, and maintain structural integrity over 30+ years of service.

VAGOTECH Solution: Laminated tempered glass engineered to EN 12288. Custom curvature. UV-protective coating. Acoustic lamination reduces cab noise by up to 6dB.

  • Standard: EN 12288 (railway vehicles)
  • Type: Laminated + tempered
  • Thermal Shock: -30°C to +70°C rated
  • Impact Rating: High-energy impact resistant
  • Acoustic Option: 50+ dB noise reduction
  • Lead Time: 6-8 weeks custom

Passenger Side Glass

The Challenge: Passenger windows face constant thermal cycling, vibration, and the need for passenger comfort (noise reduction, clarity). STB tramways and CFR trains demand reliability and safety.

VAGOTECH Solution: Tempered or laminated glass per EN 14752. Custom dimensions for any rail car. Optional acoustic lamination for modern comfort. Easy-clean coatings reduce maintenance.

  • Standard: EN 14752 (railway safety)
  • Type: Tempered or laminated
  • Dimensions: Custom curves and angles
  • Acoustic: Noise reduction lamination
  • Coatings: Anti-microbial, easy-clean options
  • Lead Time: 6-8 weeks

Interior Cabin Glass

The Challenge: Interior partitions, crew areas, emergency windows need impact resistance, durability, and low maintenance. Crew safety is paramount. Modern passengers expect good visibility.

VAGOTECH Solution: Tempered or laminated interior glass per EN 12600. Anti-scratch coatings. Frosted or clear finishes. Lightweight for energy efficiency. 50+ year lifespan.

  • Standard: EN 12600 (impact certification)
  • Finishes: Clear, frosted, tinted
  • Impact Resistance: High-velocity certified
  • Coatings: Anti-scratch, antimicrobial
  • Durability: 50+ year lifecycle
  • Lead Time: 6-8 weeks
