The VAGOTECH Story

European-Engineered Glass for a Railway Network in Full Modernization

VAGOTECH is the railway and tramway division of Glas Expert — a European architectural glass manufacturer with 25+ years of experience and a place among the Top 20 glass processors in Europe. The same precision engineering proven on landmark projects across the continent is now focused on a single mission: supplying certified rail-grade glass to the region's modernizing fleet operators.

Why now. Romania's rail network is in the middle of its largest modernization in decades. CFR Călători is introducing new Alstom Coradia Stream electric trainsets and Traxx locomotives. Bucharest's STB has deployed new trams and has contracted dozens more. Hungary's MÁV is replacing aging EMU fleets. New rolling stock and refurbishment programmes across 7 countries all share one requirement: certified, dimensionally exact glass delivered on a dependable schedule.

The challenge operators face. Rail glass must survive thermal cycling, vibration, impact and decades of daily duty — while meeting non-negotiable European standards: EN 15152 for windscreens, EN 15153 for side and door glass, EN 12600 for impact performance, and — critically for EU tendering — EN 45545 for fire and smoke behaviour. A single certification gap or a slipped delivery can hold an entire vehicle in the workshop. Operators need a partner that treats glass as a precision component, not a commodity.

The VAGOTECH advantage. In-house tempering, lamination, curved shaping and custom edge work mean no outsourcing and no compromises. We engineer to exact OEM and operator specifications, document full compliance including EN 45545, and deliver in 4–6 weeks — against the industry standard of 12–16 weeks from larger European suppliers. European quality, regional proximity, single-supplier accountability.

Windscreen Glass

The challenge: Driver cab windscreens on locomotives and EMUs need crystal clarity, must withstand temperature extremes, resist high-energy impacts from ballast and debris, and hold structural integrity across decades of service.

VAGOTECH solution: Laminated, heat-strengthened glass engineered to EN 15152. Custom curvature to OEM cab geometry. Optional heating layer for de-icing, UV-protective and acoustic interlayers. EN 45545 fire & smoke compliant builds available.

  • Standard: EN 15152 (rail windscreens) + EN 45545
  • Type: Laminated + heat-strengthened
  • Options: Heated / de-icing layer
  • Impact: High-energy ballast impact resistant
  • Acoustic: Noise-reduction interlayer
  • Lead Time: 4–6 weeks standard
Photo: Wikimedia Commons / CC BY 2.0

Passenger Side & Door Glass

The challenge: Passenger and door windows face constant thermal cycling and vibration while carrying comfort and safety expectations — clarity, noise reduction, and certified impact behaviour. Trams and coaches run these windows for decades.

VAGOTECH solution: Tempered or laminated glazing to EN 15153 and EN 12600 impact class. EN 45545 compliant builds available where required for EU tendering. Custom dimensions, curves and angles for any car type.

  • Standard: EN 15153 + EN 12600 + EN 45545
  • Type: Tempered or laminated
  • Dimensions: Custom curves and angles
  • Acoustic: Noise-reduction interlayer
  • Coatings: Easy-clean / low-maintenance
  • Lead Time: 4–6 weeks standard
Photo: Wikimedia Commons / CC BY-SA 4.0

Interior & Partition Glass

The challenge: Interior partitions, crew screens and emergency windows need certified impact resistance, durability and low maintenance — while meeting fire and smoke requirements that are increasingly enforced across EU rolling stock.

VAGOTECH solution: Tempered or laminated interior glass to EN 12600 impact classification and EN 45545 fire behaviour. Clear, frosted or tinted finishes. Anti-scratch coatings. Lightweight builds for vehicle efficiency.

  • Standard: EN 12600 + EN 45545 (fire & smoke)
  • Finishes: Clear, frosted, tinted
  • Impact: Classified safety behaviour
  • Coatings: Anti-scratch, easy-clean
  • Weight: Optimised for vehicle efficiency
  • Lead Time: 4–6 weeks standard
